Who We Are

Rwandan Alliance for Change (RAC/ARC) is an advocacy movement committed to human rights, equality, freedom, justice, and accountable governance.

We believe every Rwandan should have the opportunity to understand, exercise, and defend their rights and participate meaningfully in the future of their country.

Learn More

Our Mission

We advocate for a Rwanda where human rights are respected, citizens are treated equally before the law, and governance serves the interests of the people.

Protect Human Rights

Advocate for fundamental freedoms, dignity, and equal rights for every Rwandan.

Promote Democracy & Equality

Support accountable governance, civic participation, and equality before the law.

Advocate for Peaceful Change

Address injustice and promote meaningful change toward a peaceful and democratic Rwanda.

Our Principles

Freedom

The right to express ideas, participate, and speak without fear.

Equality

Equal rights and equal protection under the law.

Justice

Fairness, accountability, and respect for human dignity.

Democracy

Meaningful participation in decisions that shape society.

Peace

Learning from history and addressing injustice to help prevent future violence.
